Our services

One partner from transfer to final repayment. Use one service or combine them; each arrangement is scoped and priced around the portfolio.

Portfolio acquisition

Sell a loan book, or a defined part of it, and receive the purchase price up front.

  • Consumer and business loan portfolios
  • Whole books or selected segments and vintages
  • Pricing built on loan-level data

Best forLenders exiting a product or market, or releasing capital for new lending.

Portfolio management

We run acquired portfolios through to final repayment.

  • Loan administration and borrower communication
  • Collections and debt recovery, starting with amicable solutions
  • Onward sale of portfolio segments where appropriate

Best forPortfolios that need active, long-term attention after transfer.

Credit protection

Protect new loans against credit losses for an agreed fee.

  • Cover set per lender, product and portfolio
  • Eligibility, limits and fees agreed in contract
  • Lenders keep originating with a known cost of risk

Best forGrowing lenders that want predictable credit costs.

What we review before making an offer

Before we make an offer we review the portfolio at loan level. A complete data pack shortens the process. These are the first items we ask for.

  1. 1
    Loan-level data tapeBalance, interest rate, term, origination date and current status for every loan.
  2. 2
    Performance by vintageArrears buckets (1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days past due), defaults and recoveries.
  3. 3
    Cash collection historyMonthly collections since origination, split by vintage.
  4. 4
    Underwriting policyScorecards, affordability checks and how they have changed over time.
  5. 5
    Servicing set-upWho services the loans today, on which systems, and how borrowers are contacted.
  6. 6
    Legal documentationLicences, loan agreement templates and assignment terms.

How we work

Four stages from first call to transfer, each with a clear output, so both sides know what comes next.

  1. Introduction

    Tell us about the portfolio and what you want to achieve. We sign an NDA before any data changes hands.

  2. Data review

    We work through the data pack, test it against our model and come back with questions.

  3. Terms

    We agree price or fee, scope and responsibilities, and set them out in contract.

  4. Transfer and management

    The portfolio moves to Cleyve, or cover starts on new loans. Reporting follows the agreed schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Who does Cleyve work with?

Banks and non-bank lenders in Europe that want to sell a loan portfolio, hand over its management or protect new lending. Investors can fund Cleyve through the Hive5 platform.

How is Cleyve connected to Hive5?

Cleyve's portfolio purchases are funded by a business loan from Hive Finance UAB. Hive5 makes claim rights on that loan available to investors and passes on principal and interest as they are repaid. Terms are set out in the investment documentation on Hive5.

Which loans are in scope?

Consumer and business loan portfolios. We assess each portfolio's market, loan characteristics and documentation individually before making an offer.

What does portfolio management include?

Loan administration, borrower communication, collections and debt recovery, and onward sale of portfolio segments where appropriate. The scope is agreed for each arrangement.

How does credit protection work?

A partner lender pays an agreed fee, and Cleyve covers credit losses on eligible new loans within the agreed terms. Eligibility, coverage, fees and limits are set in a contract with each lender.

Where is Cleyve based, and who owns it?

Cleyve is registered in Latvia and wholly owned by Hive Finance UAB. Its first activity covers portfolios in Spain and Poland.
